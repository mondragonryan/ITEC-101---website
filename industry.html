About the Industry

BetterHelp is an online therapy and tele-mental-health platform that connects clients with licensed therapists through secure messaging, video, and phone sessions. The industry sits at the intersection of healthcare, technology, and consumer services — managing sensitive personal health information (PHI) while delivering accessible care at scale.

My Role — Clinical Research Coordinator

As a Clinical Research Coordinator at BetterHelp I support research studies that evaluate treatment outcomes, platform effectiveness, and new care models. My responsibilities include recruiting and consenting participants, managing study data, coordinating with clinicians, ensuring protocol compliance, and preparing datasets for analysis.

Ethical Issue in the Role

A key ethical issue is safeguarding participant privacy while enabling meaningful research. Research requires access to study data, but participants expect confidentiality and informed consent. Balancing de-identification for analysis with sufficient context for valid results — and communicating data use clearly in consent forms — is an ongoing ethical responsibility.

Cybersecurity Issues That Could Arise

1) Data breaches of PHI: If research or clinical data (notes, recordings, session metadata) are exposed, participants' privacy and safety can be harmed and regulatory penalties may apply.
2) Insecure integrations & third-party risk: Telehealth platforms use multiple vendors (analytics, cloud, video providers). A vulnerable vendor or misconfigured API could expose sensitive data or provide a backdoor into systems.

Cybersecurity Best Practices for BetterHelp

• Enforce strict access control and role-based permissions (least privilege) so research and clinical staff only see data necessary for their tasks.
• Require multi-factor authentication for all staff and administrative access.
• Encrypt data at rest and in transit (end-to-end for session recordings and TLS for APIs).
• De-identify research datasets and use data-use agreements; centralize PHI access and logging to enable audits.
• Maintain vendor risk assessments, ensure third parties meet HIPAA/security requirements, and apply secure API practices.
• Keep systems patched, perform regular penetration tests, and run tabletop exercises for incident response (including ransomware recovery and breach notification).

Hardware & Tools I Work With

Laptops and tablets for data collection and coordination; secure servers and cloud storage for datasets; encrypted backups and VPN access for remote work. Office devices include printers/scanners (restricted for PHI use) and MFA-enabled admin consoles for research platforms.

Summary

Research at BetterHelp can drive improvements in mental health care, but it requires careful ethical safeguards and strong cybersecurity controls. Combining technical protections (encryption, MFA, logging) with policies (consent clarity, least privilege, vendor checks, training) protects participants while allowing meaningful, compliant research.
